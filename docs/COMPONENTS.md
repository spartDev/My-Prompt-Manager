# Component Catalog

Which component does which job in the popup and the side panel, and the conventions they share. This page does not copy Props or code: the source file is the reference. Most components declare their Props interface at the top of the file; `LibraryView`, `PromptCard`, `PromptEditor`, `SearchBar` and `SearchEmptyState` import theirs from `src/types/components.ts`. Open them before you use or change a component.

---

## Where things live

`src/App.tsx` is the shell: it holds the Prompts and Categories (`usePrompts`, `useCategories`), the Notification (`useToast`) and the current section, and renders one view plus the `TabBar`. Arriving on a Section from the tab bar slides it 26px in from the side of its tab with a fade (`animate-section-forward` or `animate-section-back`, by the order of `SECTION_ORDER`); the first Section shows at once. The editor is a sheet above them: it rises from the bottom (`animate-sheet-in`), the Section and the `TabBar` wait `inert` beneath it, receding a little under a scrim (`animate-recede-in`, `animate-scrim-in`), and on close it leaves to the bottom and unmounts after its animation (`useAnimatedPresence`), when the keyboard returns to what opened it. The same `App` runs in the popup and in the side panel; it receives the context as a prop. While the launch decision shows the Welcome, `App` renders `WelcomeHost` alone, with no Section and no tab bar, and the Notification in it (a refused add of the Packs keeps the Welcome on screen), wrapped, in the Welcome's flow under its header, so it covers neither the brand, the close button nor the step's title (`ToastContainer`'s `wrap` and `inFlow`, passed as `WelcomeHost`'s `notification`; My-Prompt-Manager-ojz.20, My-Prompt-Manager-5lf). The primary button of the Welcome's last step leaves it through `useWelcomeExit`: the Packs added, then the Library on All with a Notification of what was added and the keyboard on the first card's Copy, so one Enter copies; or, with no Pack, the Library, then the editor on a new Prompt 250ms later, the keyboard in its title field. The failure Notification of a refused add stays until the user acts; an exit that starts hides it, and so does a step change (`WelcomeHost`'s `onStepChange`), so it never stays over the Library with a Try again that does nothing, nor over another step's controls; a Pack toggled hides it too (`onPacksChange`, only that Notification, `isAddFailure`), as its Try again was about the Packs refused, and moves nothing, the Welcome keeping the Notification's room while the step stays (My-Prompt-Manager-5lf). While the Packs are being added, `App` locks the Pack toggles (`WelcomeHost`'s `packsLocked`), so a refusal's Try again adds the Packs on screen. A Try again refused again gives the keyboard to the new Notification's Try again (`focusAction`), its own having left with the old one. A failed read of the Library shows Error Loading Data and its Try again in place of the Section and the tab bar: see [App: Error Loading Data and Try again](#app-error-loading-data-and-try-again).

## Which component for which job

### App: Error Loading Data and Try again

A failed read of the Library, its Prompts or its Categories, replaces the Section and the tab bar with Error Loading Data and its Try again (My-Prompt-Manager-ojz.22, My-Prompt-Manager-ou48, My-Prompt-Manager-p4y9). How the busy button looks, and why it is held, is "Loading State" in [DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md](./DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md).

- **Retry and busy state**
  - Try again reads the Prompts and the Categories again. The screen stays until both reads have ended.
  - While they run, Try again is busy: the spinner and "Trying again...", `aria-disabled`, never `disabled`. A press does nothing.
  - Reads that work show the Section at once.
  - Reads that fail again keep it busy, over the error it tries past, until `RETRY_FAILURE_HOLD_MS` (300 ms) has passed since the press. Then the new error, the idle Try again and the status change together.
  - A read still pending after `RETRY_READ_BOUND_MS` (10 s) gives the button back. The reads go on, and their outcome applies if they settle.
  - Both constants are in `src/constants/ui.ts`.
- **Status announcement**
  - The outcome is said in a `role="status"` (`load-status`): "Your library is back", "Still couldn't read your library", or, past `RETRY_READ_BOUND_MS`, "Still reading your library. You can try again."
  - It comes second in both the error screen and the Section's tree, right after the screen's own element, so React keeps the one element from one to the other.
  - It is never first. Placed first, its sr-only box (1px, margin -1px) would sit at -1px, where the side panel's editor motion would measure it for the app. Second, it sits at the bottom edge, and the app stays the root's first element.
- **Focus destinations** (never the document on the way)
  - While the reads run, and after reads that fail again: Try again keeps the keyboard. When the reads work, it stays there until the Section that comes back takes it.
  - The Library back: its active Library filter, All at an opening, or with no Prompt the main action of its empty state.
  - Another Section back: its tab (`TabBar`'s `focusActive`).
- **The Welcome exit**
  - The Welcome's exit asked for the first card's Copy and the Library has not shown since: that Copy, instead of the filter (`LibraryView`'s `focusFirstPrompt`).
  - With no card left, every Prompt deleted from another surface meanwhile: the main action of the empty Library.

### Analytics charts

`DailyUseChart` and `TimeOfDayChart` (`src/components/analytics/charts/`) are the two charts of the full-page view, both drawn by `PillBarChart`: plain elements, no chart library. A pill bar per entry in the accent (`bg-accent`), sized against the busiest and growing from the bottom like the hero's, axis labels in `text-tertiary` (Daily use names every seventh day back from today), a muted band and a `ChartTooltip` in the hero's dark pill on hover, data from `useUsageStats`, and an `aria-label` that says what the bars show. Everything else on both surfaces is plain elements too: the weekday bars of `StatsHero`, and the ranked rows of `MostUsedCard` and `RankedRows` (`RankRowContent`), computed by `src/services/usageInsights.ts`. The row counts are in `src/components/analytics/constants.ts`: 3 Most used rows in the tab, 10 in the full-page view, 6 per Platforms or Categories card.

## Conventions

- **Colors:** token classes only (`bg-card`, `text-secondary`, `border-default`…), never a raw palette class or a `dark:` color variant. The rule is in `CODING_STANDARDS.md`, the tokens in [DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md](./DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md), and `src/theme/__tests__/rawColors.test.ts` enforces it on migrated components and on this page.
- **Focus:** the focus classes of `tailwind.config.js` (`focus-primary`, `focus-input`…); see "Focus States" in DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md.
- **Verbatim Prompt text:** a component shows a Prompt's title and content exactly as stored. React renders them as text; nothing sanitizes or rewrites them (`CONTEXT.md`, ADR-001).
- **Forms:** submit with React 19's `useActionState`, as `src/components/PromptEditor.tsx` does (`CODING_STANDARDS.md`). Character counters read `PROMPT_LIMITS`, and storage runs Admission on save: a form never keeps its own copy of the limits or the rules.
- **Memo:** `PromptCard` uses a custom props comparison. Adding a prop to it means adding it to its comparison, or the component will not re-render when the prop changes.
- **Accessibility:** icon-only buttons carry an `aria-label`; dialogs use `useModalFocus` (`src/hooks/useModalFocus.ts`): focus moves in, Tab wraps inside, Escape closes and the focus returns to the opener; menus are keyboard-navigable (`Dropdown` does it for you).

## Adding a component

1. Put it in the folder of its area (table above), with an explicit Props interface at the top of the file.
2. Reuse `Dropdown`, `ConfirmDialog`, `Switch`, `SegmentedControl`, `ViewHeader` rather than building another menu, dialog, switch or header.
3. Style it with the token classes and the patterns of [DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md](./DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md) ("Component Patterns").
4. Test it in a `__tests__/` folder next to it ([TESTING.md](./TESTING.md)). If it is on the tokens, add it to `MIGRATED` in `src/theme/__tests__/rawColors.test.ts`.
5. Add a row to the table of this page: one line on its job, no Props.
